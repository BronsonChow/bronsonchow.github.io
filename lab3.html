Mechnical Keyboard Information Guide

Switches, Materials, Modding and more

 

Prelude

I will not be addressing the question why do I need one because it is already assumed you are interested in mechanical keyboards. I am not here to convince anyone to buy one. This page exists simply as a hub for mechnical keyboard information. Some sections will have a succinct description as to not overbloat the page. More in-depth and better information can be found elsewhere.

Type : Hall Effect v Mechanical

The two types of keyboards that will be the topic of this page will be the traditional mechanical and Hall Effect. Hall Effect keyboards are generally more durable and reliable due to the fewer points of contact/friction. Effectively though, durability is a non-issue when it comes to either one of these two options. The difference lies in the analog switches in Hall Effect keyboards. Rapid trigger allows for instant re-actuation of switches and you are also able to set the actuation point per-key. Otherwise, more companies offer more customization options for traditional mechanical keyboards.
