1. Type : Hall v Mechanical

The two types of keyboards that will be the topic of this page will be the traditional mechanical and Hall Effect. Hall Effect keyboards are generally more durable and reliable due to the fewer points of contact/friction. Effectively though, durability is a non-issue when it comes to either one of these two options. The difference lies in the analog switches in Hall Effect keyboards. Rapid trigger allows for instant re-actuation of switches and you are also able to set the actuation point per-key. Otherwise, generally more companies offer more customization options for traditional mechanical keyboards.
